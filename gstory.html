green boys story : The Ultras Green Boys 2005 (GB05) are the founding fathers of the Moroccan ultra movement. Supporting Raja Club Athletic 🇲🇦 out of Casablanca, they reshaped fan culture across the Kingdom and North Africa. Before 2005, Moroccan stadium support consisted of informal fan clubs. Inspired by European (Italian) and North African (Tunisian) ultra scenes, a passionate group of young Raja fans gathered in June 2005 to form Green Boys 2005, officially introducing the Ultra mentality to Morocco. 🎭 Visual & Tifo Artistry : Pioneering 3D Displays: The Green Boys were among the first in Africa to implement complex 3D tifos, using rigged ropes and pulleys to elevate massive artwork above the stands alongside coordinated roll-out banners. Choreography & Pyrotechnics: Matches against l rivals like Wydad (the Casablanca Derby) or in major CAF Champions League fixtures feature precisely timed smoke displays, flares, and section-wide card stunts (carégraphie). 📢 A Platform for Social & Political Voice : F'bladi Dhalmouni ("In My Country, I Am Oppressed"): Composed and popularized by Raja's supporters, this chant captured the frustration, economic hardship, and social disillusionment of North African youth. It spread rapidly across social media, becoming a broader anthem for social justice beyond the stadium walls. Global Solidarity: The Curva Sud routinely displays massive tifos, banners, and chants in support of international causes, most notably expressing solidarity with Palestine. Voice of the Unheard: For many young Casawis, the stadium serves as a rare space where they can collectively express grievances, identity, and hope. 🛡️ Strict Ultra Principles : Independent Financing: Following classic Ultra philosophy, the Green Boys reject financial contributions from Raja CA’s board, political parties, or corporate sponsors. Self-Sufficiency: All materials for banners, flags, and tifos are funded strictly through internal member dues, sales of official group merchandise (stickers, t-shirts, hoodies), and community donations. Autonomy: Maintaining financial independence ensures their chants, banners, and messages remain completely unedited and autonomous.
